Privacy Policy

How Nakband Welfare Foundation collects, uses, protects, and deletes personal data in the Nakband Welfare Foundation Android application.

App: Nakband Welfare Foundation Package: com.nakband.welfare Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Who We Are

Nakband Welfare Foundation (Nakband, Seni Gumbat, Kohat, Pakistan) operates the Nakband Welfare Foundation mobile application to coordinate community welfare aid and voluntary blood donation support.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Guest Access: You may browse approved blood donors and Kohat City blood-providing organizations without signing in. No account data is collected in Guest Mode.
  • Account Information (Google Sign-In): When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and unique account identifier (Firebase UID) to manage your profile and role permissions.
  • Voluntary Blood Donor Details (Optional): If you register or are listed as a blood donor with your consent, we store your full name, gender, age, blood group, village, district, residential address, phone number, WhatsApp availability, availability status, and optional last donation date.
  • Welfare Beneficiary & Tailoring Records (Authorized Staff Only): Authorized foundation administrators, managers, and village tailors record beneficiary details (name, family/address details, age, gender, and clothing measurements) strictly for charitable clothing distribution and welfare aid.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display approved voluntary blood donors (name, blood group, village/district, and availability) in the community blood donor directory.
  • To display your phone number and WhatsApp contact link only when you explicitly opt in to share contact details.
  • To coordinate community welfare aid and clothing distribution through authorized foundation staff and tailors.
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, and synchronize updates with offline caching.

No Ads & No Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and the app does not display third-party advertisements.

4. Data Storage, Security & Third-Party Services

Data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase App Check) with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and strict role-based security rules. Local on-device caching is enabled via Cloud Firestore so previously loaded records remain accessible when offline.

External links such as phone dialer, WhatsApp, or Google Maps directions open your device's native apps only when you tap them.

5. Community Directory & Non-Medical Disclaimer

The Nakband Welfare Foundation app is a voluntary community welfare and blood-donor contact directory. It does not provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or blood screening/testing services. Always consult licensed hospitals and certified blood banks for medical screening and blood transfusions.

6. Children's Privacy

Account sign-in and voluntary blood donor registration are intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child's personal data was entered without guardian consent, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

7. Your Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You can update your donor profile, toggle phone number visibility, or permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  1. Immediate In-App Deletion: Open the Nakband Welfare Foundation app → sign in → open Profile → tap Delete Account and confirm. This permanently removes your account profile, blood donor application, and public blood donor directory listing.
  2. Email Deletion Request: If you cannot access the app, email mud4sir123789@gmail.com from your registered Google email address (or include your registered name and phone number) with the subject "Delete My Account". We will permanently delete your account and donor records within 7 business days.

Note: Only authorized foundation welfare records required for ongoing charitable accountability may be retained internally if separately recorded by foundation staff, unless deletion is requested by the individual or guardian.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, privacy concerns, or data removal requests, please contact:
